# Primal: The Hunter's Journal

## Product

A table-side companion for *Primal: The Awakening*. It removes campaign bookkeeping while combat, cards, miniatures, and monster behavior remain physical.

## Users

- New players need explicit next actions and enough context to avoid campaign-state mistakes.
- Experienced groups need fast access to hunter resources, loadouts, quest state, and campaign records without leaving play.

## Core jobs

1. Guide the group through each chapter phase.
2. Record campaign decisions and outcomes safely.
3. Maintain hunter profiles, campaign-granted card-pool upgrades, resources, equipment, potions, and notes.
4. Surface only information relevant to the current table task.

## Usage context

The primary device is a laptop or tablet beside a crowded game board. Phones are supported for reference and bookkeeping. The interface must remain readable at arm’s length, keyboard accessible, touch friendly, and resilient to interrupted sessions.

## Principles

- The next required action is always obvious.
- Chapter play, hunter management, and campaign records are separate workspaces.
- Detail appears on demand unless it requires a player decision.
- Saved state and destructive transitions are explicit.
- The physical game remains the focus.
