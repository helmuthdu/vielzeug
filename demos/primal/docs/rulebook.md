## Overview

**Primal: The Awakening** is a cooperative game for 1–5 players set in an epic fantasy world where humans struggle to survive in an untamed wilderness. Players take on the role of hunters battling monstrous creatures, embarking on quests to defend humanity and uncover the mystery of The Awakening.

The game can be played in three modes:

- **Prologue**: A tutorial scenario that teaches the basics of gameplay and serves as the first scenario of Campaign mode.
- **Campaign Mode**: A series of multiple game sessions linked by an overarching storyline, featuring 11 chapters, 30+ quests, crafting, and character progression.
- **Expedition Mode**: Standalone, one-shot scenarios for quick play with customizable hunters and equipment.

**Note**: The *Primal: The Awakening* base game is required to play any expansion.

## Core Concepts

### The Monster

Each monster is represented by a unique miniature and a monster board featuring:

- **Monster's name**
- **Trophy icon** (used to identify components)
- **Monster element** (large icon) and **elemental weaknesses** (smaller icons)
- **Health track** (monster starts with 10 health)
- **Behavior slots** (×3, or ×6 for The Awakened)

**Components**:
- **Behavior deck**: Main monster deck determining unique moves during combat.
- **Stance deck**: Stance cards represent different stages of combat (I, II, III, or V for The Awakened). As the monster suffers wounds, it progresses through stances.
- **Peril deck**: Special abilities relating to various stances.
- **Objective card**: Optional bonuses for players.
- **Attrition deck**: Cards used during the Attrition phase.
- **Struggle tokens**: Represent the intensifying rage of the monster.

### The Hunters

Each hunter is represented by a unique miniature and a hunter board featuring spaces for equipment, action deck, and discard pile.

**Hunter Components**:
- **Action deck**: Offensive and defensive moves.
- **Mastery card**: Signature ability unlocked during combat.
- **Equipment and Potion cards**: Weapon, armor, helm, potions, and special items.

**Base Game Hunters**:

| Hunter | Class | Weapon | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dareon** | Great Sword | Great Sword | Crushing blows, combos, Vulnerable exploitation |
| **Mirah** | Great Bow | Great Bow | Versatile, low-cost cards, Stealth, Blind, Volley |
| **Thoreg** | Hammer | Hammer | Control-oriented, Stun, Confuse, struggle removal |
| **Ljonar** | Sword and Shield | Sword and Shield | Team support, tank, Taunt, Retaliate, Resilience |

**Expansion Hunters**:

| Hunter | Class | Weapon | Expansion | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Drusk** | Wardrum | Wardrum | Heart of the Wild | Support, Rhythm track, Resonance, card draw |
| **Zaraya** | Spear | Great Spear | Heart of the Wild | Attack-oriented, Pierce, Guard, Reclaim |
| **Heleren** | Heavy Gun | Gunbow | Mount Havoc | Bullet resource, Reload, control effects |
| **Karah** | Dual Blade | Dual Blade | Mount Havoc | Battle Dance, Chain, Overrun, Stealth |

### Combat Board

The combat board represents the location where the fight takes place. The monster always occupies the center. The board is divided into four sectors:

- **Front** (bottom)
- **Rear** (top)
- **Flank** (left and right)

**Miniature Base Orientation**: The monster miniature's base has raised segments indicating its front side. When the monster rotates, sectors are redefined based on their corresponding side of the monster.

### Player Count Icon

The [Player Count] icon refers to the number of players who started the scenario. The designation X indicates an amount equal to X multiplied by the player count.

## Gameplay Rules

### Round Structure

A round consists of the following steps in order:

1. **Consume**: Each player may resolve one Consume ability.
2. **Monster Upkeep**:
    - **Behavior Refresh** (skip round 1): Discard the behavior with the lowest refresh value and replace it.
    - **Struggle**: Monster gains 1 [Struggle] per player.
3. **Player Turns**: Players take turns clockwise starting with the first player (aggro player).
4. **End of Round**: Resolve all "at the end of the round" effects. Advance round marker.

### Player's Turn Structure

1. **Movement Phase**: Move to an adjacent sector (costs 1 [Stamina]) or stay (become threatened).
2. **Action Phase**: Play cards, activate abilities, Revive.
3. **Attrition Phase**: Check for attrition damage.
4. **End of Turn**: Discard sequence, refill hand, monster rotates to aggro player.

### Movement Phase

- **Move**: Pay 1 [Stamina] to place your miniature in an adjacent sector. Remove threatened token.
- **Stay**: Become threatened and take a threatened token.

### Action Phase

During your Action phase, you may perform any of the following actions in any order (except Revive, once per turn):

- Play an action card from your hand.
- Activate an ability that begins with the keyword **Action**.
- **Revive** (once per turn).

**Playing Action Cards**: You can play up to 5 action cards (standard sequence limit) from your hand, resolving them one at a time. The row of cards played left to right is your **sequence**.

To play a card, pay its stamina cost by discarding cards from your hand (each card generates 1 [Stamina] per [Stamina] icon).

**Card Effects by Color**:

| Card Type | Color | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Attack** | Red | Deal damage equal to weapon damage. Only playable if in a sector on an exposed side. |
| **Maneuver** | Blue | Remove 1 struggle token from the monster board. |
| **Parry** | Yellow | No additional effect; resolve ability text only. |
| **Dodge** | Green | No additional effect; resolve ability text only. |

**Aggro Cards**: Cards with the red aggro icon [Aggro]. When you play an aggro card, immediately take the aggro token.

### Attrition Phase

1. Reveal the top card of the attrition deck.
2. Suffer attrition damage unless you have a number of defensive cards ([Parry] or [Dodge]) in your sequence equal to or higher than the attrition value. Attrition damage equals the monster's damage value.
3. Discard the attrition card.

**If Threatened**: Reveal two attrition cards and use the value that gives the worst outcome.

### End of Turn

1. Discard your sequence (last card played becomes top of discard pile).
2. Refill your hand to your hand size limit (default 5).
3. Monster rotates to face the aggro player.

### Monster Reaction System

There are always three behavior cards in play in the behavior slots. Each behavior card has a reaction icon on the back representing a triggering condition.

**Behavior Card Resolution**:

1. **Reaction**: Resolve the card's base ability text and boost effect (if any).
2. **Rampage**: Trigger any rampage cards in play.
3. **Discard and Replenish**: Discard the behavior card and refill the slot.

### Boost Effect

Some behavior cards feature a boost effect. To resolve it, remove struggle tokens equal to the boost cost and resolve the boost ability text.

### Escalation

When the behavior deck is empty, reshuffle the behavior discard pile to form a new deck. The monster gains 1 struggle. This is called escalation.

### Damage and Wounds

**Damage to Monster**: Place damage tokens on the stance card. When damage equals the toughness value, inflict a wound (reduce monster health by 1, remove damage tokens equal to toughness).

**Stance Progression**: When monster health reaches the progression value, advance to the next stance.

**Fatigue Damage**: When you empty your deck, suffer damage equal to your weapon level.

### KO'd and Rise

When your total sustained damage reaches or exceeds your health value, you are KO'd:

1. End your turn immediately.
2. Remove all damage tokens.
3. Discard your hand and sequence.
4. Add a Wound card to your discard pile and reshuffle.
5. Place a deplete token on your armor or helm.
6. Lay your miniature down.
7. Take a KO token (red side up).
8. Monster turns to aggro player.

**Rise**: When it would be your turn and your KO token is black side up, discard it, draw up to hand size limit, stand your miniature up. Play passes to the next player.

### Revive Action

Once per turn, during your Action phase, if you are in a sector with a KO'd player, you may pay 2 [Stamina] to flip that player's KO token to its black side.

### Card Resolution Timing

When multiple card abilities trigger simultaneously, resolve in this order:

1. Stance cards
2. Peril cards
3. Behavior cards
4. Other cards

### Success and Failure

- **Success**: Reduce the monster's health to 0.
- **Failure**: All players are KO'd, or you reach the end of round 10.

## Keywords & Icons

### Color Icons

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Attack] | Attack card (red action card) |
| [Maneuver] | Maneuver card (blue action card) |
| [Parry] | Parry card (yellow action card) |
| [Dodge] | Dodge card (green action card) |
| [Offensive] | Offensive action card (Maneuver or Attack) |
| [Defensive] | Defensive action card (Parry or Dodge) |

### General Icons

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Player Count] | Number of players who started the scenario |
| [Player Count -1] | Number of players minus one |
| [Half Player Count] | Half the number of players (rounded down) |
| [Stamina] | Stamina resource |
| [Weapon Level] | Your weapon level |
| [Aggro] | Aggro card |
| [Monster Damage] | Monster's damage value |
| [Monster Damage ×2] | Double the monster's damage |

### Keywords (Alphabetical)

**Acceleration token**: Place on monster board. During Monster Upkeep, monster gains 1 additional struggle per acceleration token.

**Action: [Ability]**: Resolve as an action during your Action phase.

**Active player**: The player currently playing their turn.

**Adjacent sector**: Two sectors sharing a common border.

**Aggro player**: The player with the aggro token.

**Aim (Mirah)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Arkeum Tokens (Hurom)**: Protect Hurom from attacks. When you play an attack card in a sector with an Arkeum token, the token prevents damage and is removed. Max 1 per player per sector.

**Assist**: During another player's turn, discard a card with Assist to let the active player draw 1. Once per turn per player.

**Attrition check**: Reveal top card of attrition deck. Suffer attrition damage unless you have enough defensive cards. If threatened, reveal two cards and use worse outcome.

**Avoid attrition damage**: Not suffering attrition damage during your Attrition phase.

**Ballista Tokens (The Awakened)**: Placed in corners. Activate at end of round as per Artillery Assault objective. Can be loaded (black) or unloaded (grey).

**Base weapon damage**: Printed damage value on your weapon card.

**Battle Dance (Karah)**: When you play consecutive [Attack] cards in sequence, deal damage at 2nd (3×[Weapon Level]), 4th (5×[Weapon Level]), and 6th (7×[Weapon Level]) cards. Max 6 cards counted.

**Behavior card in play**: Occupies a behavior slot on the monster board.

**Berserker**: When you play a card with Berserker, enter berserker state until end of turn. Draw 1 each time you play an attack card while in this state.

**Blank**: Treat card as if it had no ability text. Traits still count.

**Bleeding Cards (Xitheros, The Awakened)**: Shuffled into action deck. When drawn, discard immediately; Xitheros gains 1 struggle (or The Awakened gains 1 struggle).

**Blind**: Choose a peril card in play. That peril is blank until start of your next turn. Use blind token.

**Bullet (Heleren)**: Resource tracked on Bullet track. Spend bullets to trigger effects. Reload X to retrieve X bullets.

**Bullet track level**: Number of bullets currently available (indicated by bullet marker).

**Burning**: Take burning token. Next time you empty your deck, discard burning token and suffer damage equal to weapon level. If already burning, discard top 5 cards of deck instead.

**Cancel the effects**: Skip the Reaction step of a behavior card's resolution.

**Captured**: Discard hand and sequence, place miniature on monster board, end turn. At start of next turn: place in front sector, draw up to hand size, suffer damage equal to weapon level, discard top 2 cards (if [Attack] discarded, play normally; otherwise skip turn).

**Chain [Color]**: Play without paying stamina cost if played after a [Color] card in your sequence.

**Claw (Kharja)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Confuse**: Place confused token on monster board, turn monster to a sector of your choice. Next boost effect is cancelled. Discard at start of next round.

**Consume: [Ability]**: Resolve during Consume phase. Once per player per round. Remove card from game after use.

**Copy**: Resolve the copied card's ability as part of the copy effect.

**Counter**: Use counter tokens when instructed.

**Critical health**: Health reduced to half value (rounded down) or less.

**Current attrition level**: Attrition value of top card of attrition discard pile.

**Dazed**: Take dazed token. Next time you play a [Maneuver] card, discard token and consider that card blank.

**Debility Card (Orouxen)**: Clogs deck. Place in sequence at start of Action phase. Counts toward sequence limit. Discard at end of turn like action cards.

**Defense token**: Counts as a defensive card for attrition check. Discard at end of attrition check.

**Deplete token**: Placed on armor/helm when KO'd. Depleted equipment's health points don't count. If both depleted, out of game.

**Disrupt token**: Place on defensive card in sequence during Attrition phase. That card doesn't count for attrition check. Discard at end of Attrition phase.

**Dust Token (Toramat, Dygorax)**: At start of Attrition phase, take a disrupt token for each Dust in your sector. Max 2 per sector. Remove all Dust at end of round.

**Empty (deck)**: When you draw/discard last card of deck. Suffer fatigue damage, reshuffle discard pile.

**Empty (hand)**: Zero cards in hand after playing/discarding.

**Enhancement: [Ability]**: Resolve along with behavior card's base ability as a modification.

**Escalation**: When behavior deck is empty, reshuffle discard pile and monster gains 1 struggle.

**Escalation: [Ability]**: Trigger when monster escalates.

**Evasion (Nagarjas)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Exhaust**: Place exhausted token. Card is blank for rest of scenario.

**Exile**: Put card in exile zone (outside field of play). Interactions only by card abilities.

**Exposed side**: Sectors where attack cards can be played. Shown in red on stance card exposure diagram.

**Fatigue damage**: Damage equal to weapon level when you empty your deck.

**Finisher**: After playing, your Action phase ends.

**Firestorm (Vyraxen)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**First player**: Aggro player becomes first player after Monster Upkeep. Takes first player token.

**Frost (Mamuraak)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Glaciation Track (Mamuraak)**: Tracks glaciation level. Increase by X spaces when instructed.

**Guard**: When another player in your sector or adjacent plays a [Defensive] card, discard a card with Guard to reduce its stamina cost to 0. Once per turn.

**Hand size limit**: Maximum cards in hand at end of turn (default 5).

**Hardened**: When monster is hardened: excess damage is not retained; cannot be stunned, confused, or inflicted with Vulnerable.

**Hardening Track (Toramat, Dygorax)**: Tracks hardening level. At level 3, monster becomes Hardened.

**Heal X**: Remove X damage tokens from your hunter board.

**Health (Monster)**: Starts at 10. Reduce by 1 per wound. At 0, monster defeated.

**Health value/Health points (Hunter)**: Sum of health points of helm and armor cards.

**Immune to blind**: Cannot be targeted by blind effect.

**Impetus (Thoreg)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Infuriate X/Y/Z**: During Monster Upkeep, monster gains additional struggle based on aggro cards in players' hands.

**KO'd / KO token**: When damage reaches health value. Follow KO steps. While KO'd, cannot be targeted (except Revive).

**Lava Geyser (Taraska)**: Discard top card of behavior deck, place fire terrain in sector indicated on Lava diagram.

**Legendary**: Equipment is effective against all monsters regardless of elemental weakness.

**Lose focus**: Flip mastery card to unfocused side.

**Move/Movement**: Place miniature in adjacent sector. Costs 1 stamina unless specified.

**Niz-Maraga Biome**: At round marker advance, all players must recycle a card matching the color shown for current round. If cannot, suffer damage equal to Aggression Level.

**Opening: [Ability]**: Triggered only if card is first in sequence.

**Overrun**: Place miniature in opposite sector. Remove threatened status. Not considered movement.

**Paralyzing Spore Cards (Pazis)**: Added to deck. While in hand, cannot move. Discard at end of Action phase.

**Permanent**: Peril/objective cards stay in play until end of scenario. Not affected by Blank.

**Piercing Damage (Zaraya)**: Special damage value used with Pierce ability.

**Primordial Blood (Xitheros)**: Special cards obtained in Campaign mode. Discard during crafting to gain 1 element resource.

**Protected side**: Sectors where attack cards cannot be played. Shown in black on exposure diagram.

**Quick Refresh**: Skip Behavior Refresh during Monster Upkeep. Perform at end of each player turn instead.

**Reaction icon**: Indicates triggering condition for behavior cards.

**Reclaim: [Condition]**: Search discard pile for card with Reclaim when condition is met. Add to hand.

**Recycle [Color]**: Discard X cards of indicated color to draw X.

**Reflexes (Hurom)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Reload (X)**: Retrieve X bullets. Move bullet marker X spaces right.

**Resilience**: After suffering [Monster Damage], reveal a card with Resilience to draw 1. Once per card per round.

**Resistant**: Do not carry excess damage over to next stance.

**Resonance**: Card trait. Triggers Drusk's Resonate ability.

**Resonate (Drusk)**: When you play a card with Resonance, resolve its effect twice if previous card also has Resonance.

**Retaliate [Color]: [Ability]**: When you suffer [Monster Damage], reveal [Color] to trigger [Ability]. Once per damage instance.

**Reveal an action card**: Show card to other players without playing it.

**Revive**: Action to help KO'd players recover. Pay 2 stamina if in same sector.

**Rhythm (Drusk)**: Track values 0–3. Increase by 1 when you discard a card matching top of discard pile. At 3, draw 1 instead. Expend to grant Inspiration bonus.

**Riding check**: Reveal top card of attrition deck and action deck. If stamina icons ≥ attrition value, deal weapon damage and choose sector; otherwise suffer [Monster Damage] and place in front sector.

**Rise**: Discard KO token, draw up to hand size, stand miniature up.

**Rampage**: Behavior cards with rampage icon. Trigger during Rampage step.

**Sequence**: Row of cards played left to right. Standard limit is 5.

**Setup: [Instructions]**: Resolve at beginning of scenario.

**Shield (X)**: Reduce damage to monster by X per source.

**Slash (Dareon)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Slow**: Place slowed token on monster board. Do not refill behavior slot when resolving behavior card. Remove at end of your turn.

**Source of damage**: Any effect causing damage.

**Stamina (Generate stamina)**: Discard cards from hand to generate stamina. Each card generates 1 per [Stamina] icon.

**Stamina (Stamina cost)**: Pay by generating stamina. Printed cost of X means you choose the value.

**Stamina token**: Place on hunter board. Discard to generate 1 stamina. Max 1 unless specified.

**Stance II/III: [Ability]**: Resolve only if monster is in Stance II or III.

**Standing player**: Player not currently KO'd.

**Start: [Ability]**: Triggered at start of your turn.

**Stealth**: Card does not trigger reaction icons containing color or card type icons.

**Strain token**: Discard all strain tokens when refilling hand; draw one fewer card per token.

**Struggle**: Monster resource. Accumulates during Monster Upkeep. At 3×[Player Count], monster unleashes.

**Stun**: Place stun token on behavior card. That card cannot be triggered this round. Choose a player to reveal up to two attack cards and deal weapon damage per card revealed.

**Swarm Card (Ozew)**: While in play, cannot damage Ozew. Damage targets Swarm card instead. Defeat by dealing cumulative damage equal to Ozew's toughness value.

**Target**: Element directed by game effect.

**Taunt**: Discard a card with Taunt during another player's turn. Active player draws 1, you take aggro token, monster turns to your sector. Once per turn.

**Threatened**: Take threatened token. During attrition check, reveal 2 attrition cards and use worse outcome. Remove by moving.

**Threatened sector**: Includes at least one threatened player.

**Thornvine Tokens (Hydar)**: Increase [Stamina] cost by 1 if in sector with thornvine token.

**Toughness**: Damage needed to inflict a wound. Number on stance card × player count.

**Toxic Fume Tokens (Endless Swamp Biome)**: Draw one per player at setup. At start of each round, draw one and place in indicated sector. At end of your turn, if in sector with token, pick one up. At 3 tokens, eliminated.

**Trait**: No inherent effect, but card abilities may reference.

**Transformation Token (Nightmare Biome)**: At start of round 4, each player gains one (human side up). Flip to monster side when you play three consecutive offensive cards. Base weapon damage restored to printed value. Cannot assist or receive assists after flipping.

**Unleash**: When monster gains 3×[Player Count] struggle, immediately unleash: all players suffer [Monster Damage], remove struggle tokens until monster has 1.

**Unleash: [Ability]**: Triggered when monster unleashes.

**Unstoppable**: When behavior card is triggered, monster gains 1 struggle after resolution.

**Venom Cards (Hydar, Reikal)**: Added to deck. Accumulate in hand. Only way to remove is to use for stamina, but suffer damage equal to weapon level.

**Venomous Serpent Cards (Reikal)**: Place next to hunter board. Ability applies only to engaged player. Remove by playing a [Defensive] card in sequence without resolving ability or effect.

**Volley X**: Discard X cards from top of deck. Deal damage equal to weapon level for each offensive card ([Offensive]) discarded.

**Vulnerable**: Place vulnerable token on monster board. Discard to double damage from a single source. Cannot inflict if already vulnerable.

**Water Cycle (Korowon)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Weapon damage**: Printed damage value plus modifiers.

**Weapon level**: Shown in top right corner of weapon card.

**When focused: [Ability]**: Triggered when you focus that mastery card.

**When revealed: [Ability]**: Triggered when stance card is revealed.

**Wound/Inflict a wound/Suffer a wound**: Reduce monster health by 1. At 0 health, monster defeated.

**Zekath Variants (Zekath & Zekalith)**: Two variants with unique peril, stance, and behavior cards. Vibration track triggers special peril card replacement.

### Biome-Specific Rules

**Nightmare Biome**: Each player's base weapon damage equals weapon level (rounded down). At start of round 4, gain Transformation token (human side up). Flip to monster side by playing three consecutive offensive cards; base weapon damage restored to printed value. Cannot assist or receive assists after flipping.

**Endless Swamp Biome**: Uses bag with 16 Toxic Fume tokens. At setup, draw one per player and place in indicated sector. At start of each round, draw one token and place. At end of turn, if in sector with token, pick one up. At 3 tokens, eliminated.

**Goldarks Biome**: During rounds 2, 5, 8, 9, and 10, whenever a player or monster suffers damage, that damage is doubled. When you inflict a wound during these rounds, remove excess damage.

**Thunder Mountains Biome**: During movement phase, player suffers damage equal to monster's aggression level (minimum 1), unless they move by paying stamina cost with a [Dodge] card. During rounds 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, or 10, damage is doubled.

**Niz-Maraga Biome**: Next to each round on round track, one or two icons representing action card types. Each time round marker advances, all players must recycle a card matching the color shown. If cannot, suffer damage equal to Aggression Level. If two icons, must recycle one of each.

**Sunset Plains Biome**: On rounds 2, 5, and 8, all action cards played during that round are considered blank. Effect based on color still applies.

**Woltyar Biome**: At start of each round, each player discards top X cards of deck, where X = current round number. Lava crossing: when movement crosses lava rivers, suffer burning.

**Frozen Wastes Biome**: During rounds 1, 3, and 5, all players' mastery cards are considered blank. From round 8 onward, all mastery cards remain blank permanently.

**Crystal Caves Biome**  
Luminous crystals fall from above as the ceiling of the cavern trembles.
- This biome uses a bag with 16 colored crystal tokens (red, yellow, blue, green). During setup, shuffle all tokens and draw one per player, placing it in the sector indicated on the token’s back.
- At the start of each round, draw one token from the bag and place it in the indicated sector.
- **Crystal Tokens as Resources:** When a player pays the stamina cost to play a card, they may remove a crystal token of the same color as that card from their sector to reduce that stamina cost by 1.
- **Collapse:** At the end of round 6, the cavern collapses and the game ends. Rounds 7–10 are marked as broken apart on the round track.

**Flooded Wilds Biome**  
The tide rises, flooding the battleground and making every action more difficult.
- This biome introduces **deep-water** terrain. Use the new double-sided water tokens (water on front, deep-water on back).
- During rounds 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, and 10, the tide rises. At the start of these rounds, flip all water in play to the deep-water side. Flip them back to the water side at the end of the round.
- While a player is in a sector with deep-water terrain, their sequence limit is reduced to 3 (as regular water terrain), and when refilling their hand, their hand-size limit is reduced by 1 (draw one less card than usual).

## Monster Special Rules

### Base Game Monsters

**Charge Track (Jekoros)**: Tracks charge level. Increase by X spaces when instructed. Card abilities interact with charge level.

**Dust Token (Toramat, Dygorax)**: At start of Attrition phase, take disrupt token for each Dust in sector. Max 2 per sector. Remove all Dust at end of round.

**Hardening Track (Toramat, Dygorax)**: Tracks hardening level. At level 3, monster becomes Hardened.

**Rumble Card (Ozew)**: Indicates marked sectors. Discard previous Rumble card when new one becomes active. Reshuffle discard pile if deck empty.

**Debility Card (Orouxen)**: Place in sequence at start of Action phase. Counts toward sequence limit. Discard at end of turn.

**Swarm Card (Ozew)**: Cannot damage Ozew while in play. Damage targets Swarm card instead. Defeat by dealing cumulative damage equal to Ozew's toughness value.

**Arkeum Tokens (Hurom)**: Protect Hurom from attacks. Remove token to prevent damage. Max 1 per player per sector.

**Special Attrition Cards**: Some monsters have special attrition cards. May require specific defensive card colors.

**The Awakened**: Special rules for Ballista tokens, extended monster board (6 behavior slots), stance deck (5 stances), special attrition cards, instinct and signature behavior cards, special objective cards, sealed envelope (S3), legendary equipment set.

### Expansion Monsters

**Paralyzing Spore Cards (Pazis)**: Added to deck. While in hand, cannot move. Discard at end of Action phase.

**Evasion (Nagarjas)**: Card trait. No inherent effect.

**Quick Refresh (Zekath)**: Skip Behavior Refresh during Monster Upkeep. Perform at end of each player turn instead.

**Bleeding Cards (Xitheros, The Awakened)**: Shuffled into action deck. When drawn, discard immediately; monster gains 1 struggle.

**Primordial Blood (Xitheros)**: Special cards obtained in Campaign mode. Discard during crafting to gain 1 element resource.

**Lava Diagram (Taraska)**: Highlights specific side of monster in orange. Used by Lava Geyser keyword.

**Lava Geyser (Taraska)**: Discard top card of behavior deck, place fire terrain in sector indicated on Lava diagram.

**Special Peril Cards and Vibration Track (Zekath and Zekalith)**: Special peril cards not associated with stances. Vibration track triggers replacement of special peril cards. At vibration level 3: replace special peril card, reset track to 0.

**Glaciation Track (Mamuraak)**: Tracks glaciation level. Card abilities interact with glaciation level.

**Venom Cards (Hydar, Reikal)**: Added to deck. Accumulate in hand. Only way to remove is to use for stamina, but suffer damage equal to weapon level.

**Venomous Serpent Cards (Reikal)**: Place next to hunter board. Ability applies only to engaged player. Remove by playing a [Defensive] card in sequence without resolving ability or effect.

**Thornvine Tokens (Hydar)**: Increase [Stamina] cost by 1 if in sector with thornvine token.

**Nightmare (Variant Rule)**: Optional variant increasing difficulty. Replace standard stance cards with Nightmare variant stance cards. Add Nightmare behavior cards to behavior deck.

**Hunter's Trial and Nightmare**: Combine variants for ultimate challenge. Earn 3+X valor points per scenario completed with Nightmare variant. X = number of nightmare behavior cards included.

## Hunters

Below is a summary of each hunter’s core mechanics, unique resources, keywords, and playstyle. All hunters can be used in Campaign or Expedition mode.

### Base Game Hunters

#### Dareon – The Great Sword

- **Role:** Damage dealer focused on crushing blows and combo sequences.
- **Unique Mechanic:** Exploits the **Vulnerable** keyword to double the impact of his attacks. High stamina costs mean **cost reduction** abilities are crucial.
- **Key Keywords:** Berserker (draw 1 when playing an attack card), Resilience (after suffering monster damage, reveal to draw 1).
- **Playstyle:** Builds multiple attack sequences, uses cost reductions and free-play effects. Equipment like Dragon Claw and Wolygar Gauntlet provide tactical damage bonuses.
- **Mastery:** Relentless Assault – places counters on the card; when focused, reduces stamina cost of all attack cards by 1.

#### Mirah – The Great Bow

- **Role:** Versatile hunter with low stamina costs and strong offensive/defensive options.
- **Unique Mechanic:** Excellent **draw and recycle** effects. Can use **Stealth** to execute actions unseen and make **extra movements** during her Action phase. Uses **Blind** to neutralize peril cards and **Volley** for repeated damage.
- **Key Keywords:** Stealth, Blind, Volley, Recycle.
- **Playstyle:** Aims to empty her action deck early to focus her mastery and trigger its powerful effect. Equipment cycles through the deck faster and adds indirect damage.
- **Mastery:** Ultimate Shot – a powerful attack that scales with cards played.

#### Thoreg – The Hammer

- **Role:** Control-oriented leader who combines powerful attacks with struggle removal and assistance.
- **Unique Mechanic:** Hammer blows are devastating but come at the cost of **Strain** (reduces hand size) and needing **extra stamina** to amplify card abilities. Uses **Stun** and **Confuse** to cancel monster behavior or remove extra struggle.
- **Key Keywords:** Stun, Confuse, Strain, Struggle removal.
- **Playstyle:** Offensive strategy centered on the Massive Blow mastery, with Stun as the key to control. Skullshock helps focus mastery faster. Other equipment encourages an offensive playstyle and investing all stamina.
- **Mastery:** Massive Blow – a devastating attack that can be amplified by spending extra stamina.

#### Ljonar – Sword & Shield

- **Role:** Team support and tank, protecting allies and creating synergies.
- **Unique Mechanic:** Assumes the tank role with support efforts and impactful reactions to monster attacks. Uses **Taunt** to manipulate monster orientation. Can draw cards through **Resilience** or trigger special effects with **Retaliate**.
- **Key Keywords:** Taunt, Resilience, Retaliate, Guard.
- **Playstyle:** Executes sequences of parry and attack cards, maximizing benefits from Thundercore and Shield Master. Red Scale Armor helps with hand size; other equipment retrieves cards from discard pile.
- **Mastery:** Shield Master – enhances defensive capabilities and supports allies.

### Heart of the Wild Hunters

#### Drusk – The Wardrum

- **Role:** Support character offering control, defense, and healing.
- **Unique Mechanic:** **Rhythm track** (0–3). When you discard a card matching the color of the topmost card in your discard pile, increase rhythm level by 1 (max 3). At rhythm 3, draw 1 instead. Can expend rhythm to grant an **Inspiration Bonus** to an ally’s attack:
    - Rhythm 2: +2 × weapon level damage
    - Rhythm 3: +6 × weapon level damage
    - Resets rhythm to 0 after granting bonus.
- **Key Keywords:** Resonance (cards with this trait trigger twice if the previous card also has Resonance), Recycle.
- **Playstyle:** Built around the Thundering Drum mastery, using crystal/horn equipment to maximize draw power and cycle rapidly. Highest draw potential of all hunters when leveraging Rhythm.
- **Mastery:** Thundering Drum – enhances rhythm generation and inspiration bonuses.

#### Zaraya – The Spear

- **Role:** Attack-oriented hunter with exceptional mobility and tactical options.
- **Unique Mechanic:** **Pierce track** (0–4). Increases pierce level by playing consecutive maneuver cards. When you play an attack card at pierce level 4, you may deal **piercing damage** instead of base weapon damage. Reset pierce to 0 after the attack. Also uses **Reclaim** to retrieve offensive cards and **Guard** to protect allies.
- **Key Keywords:** Pierce, Reclaim, Guard.
- **Playstyle:** Maneuvers around the monster to find weak spots. Preconstructed deck balances Guard and Reclaim, with tools to quickly advance the Pierce track. Ideal for learning her unique playstyle.
- **Mastery:** Guardian – enhances Guard and Reclaim abilities.

### Mount Havoc Hunters

#### Heleren – The Heavy Gun

- **Role:** Versatile hunter using a unique bullet resource.
- **Unique Mechanic:** **Bullet track** (0–3). Starts with 3 bullets. Card abilities spend bullets to trigger special effects (**Ignite**), or retrieve bullets through **Reload**. Bullets power a wide spectrum of effects: attacks, team support, damage avoidance, control (Blind, Stun).
- **Key Keywords:** Bullet, Ignite, Reload.
- **Playstyle:** Preconstructed deck takes advantage of the Precision Reload mastery, enhancing the impact of her signature keyword. Equipment facilitates attrition control, card recycling, healing, and combo damage.
- **Mastery:** Precision Reload – improves bullet retrieval and attack power.

#### Karah – The Dual Blade

- **Role:** Consistent damage dealer through extended sequences.
- **Unique Mechanic:** **Battle Dance** – deals damage as she plays consecutive attack cards in her sequence. Damage triggers on the 2nd, 4th, and 6th consecutive attack cards: 3×weapon level, 5×weapon level, and 7×weapon level respectively. A full 6-card sequence deals a total of 15×weapon level damage. Additional attacks beyond the 6th do not count.
- **Key Keywords:** Battle Dance, Overrun (move to opposite sector, remove threatened), Chain [Color] (play for free after a matching color), Stealth.
- **Playstyle:** Built around the Piercing Dash mastery, exploiting Overrun for extra damage and drawing cards. Equipment helps search for and draw the right cards to create chains or offensive sequences for Battle Dance. Exceptionally mobile.
- **Mastery:** Piercing Dash – enhances mobility and damage output.

### Deckbuilding Rules

#### Action Deck Composition

Each weapon card specifies the exact number of cards of each color that must be included in your action deck. For example, the Level 1 Flame Tongue requires:

- 6 × [Attack] cards
- 4 × [Maneuver] cards
- 5 × [Parry] cards
- 5 × [Dodge] cards

**Deck Advantage:** For each 2 pieces of equipment effective against the monster (due to elemental weakness), you may add or remove 1 card of any color from your deck, modifying the color restrictions of your weapon.

#### Upgrade Cards

Upgrade cards are identified by a **Card ID** in the bottom-right corner. The letter (A, B, C, D, or E) identifies the skill tree branch, and the number (1 or 2) identifies the upgrade step.

- **First upgrade step (X1):** Unlock 2 new action cards and gain access to the next step.
- **Second upgrade step (X2):** Unlock 2 new action cards and a new mastery card.

## Campaign Rules

### Campaign Overview

A campaign is structured in 11 Chapters. Each Chapter moves the story forward and offers different quests. Players fight monsters, gather resources, upgrade equipment, and customize their hunter.

**Objective**: Complete all 11 chapters.

**Replayability**: Campaign can be replayed for different quest paths, monsters, and lore.

**Prologue**: Each new campaign begins with the Prologue scenario.

### Campaign Structure

Each chapter follows this phase structure:

1. **Story Phase**: Read chapter introduction from Campaign book. Implement instructions (level up Forge/Herbalist, card pool upgrades, unlock quests, quest expiration).
2. **Quest Board Phase**: Choose a quest from available quests on quest board. Place selected quest card in front of you as active quest.
3. **Crafting Phase**: Each player independently crafts equipment and prepares potions. Build action deck and choose up to 3 potion cards.
4. **Hunt Phase**: Play the scenario associated with the active quest. Read narrative introduction, set up game as indicated.

### Campaign Setup

1. Place quest board in center.
2. Assemble quest deck (core game + expansions).
3. Take Campaign book.
4. Take campaign sheet, circle Chapter 1.
5. Each player: take character sheet, hunter board, starting equipment, form initial card pool (starter deck + mastery card).

### Campaign Sheet

1. **Campaign Chapters track**: Track progress. Symbols indicate monster aggression level.
2. **The Herbalist**: Track Herbalist level.
3. **The Forge**: Track Forge level and unlocked forge cards.
4. **Trophies**: Track monsters defeated.
5. **Quests**: Archive quests by number. Circle unlocked, X completed/expired.
6. **Achievements**: Write unlocked achievements.
7. **Hunter's Trial**: Track Defeat track, Total Defeats, Valor points.

### Character Sheet

1. **Player name and class**.
2. **Skill tree**: Track upgrades from branches.
3. **Crafting and Plant Reserve**: Track materials, plants, elements.
4. **Notes**: Free space for notes.

### Aggression Level

Monster aggression level determined by campaign chapter:

| Chapter | Aggression Level |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1 |
| 4–7 | 2 |
| 8–11 | 3 |

### Monster Setup

- **Stance cards**: Use set for appropriate aggression level.
- **Behavior deck**: Use behavior cards for current aggression level.
- **Peril and objective cards**: Use side with correct aggression icon.

### Quest Rewards

Rewards include: new forge card, crafting resources, plant resources, achievements, new quests, new reward cards.

### Quest Expiration

Some quests expire when reaching specific chapters. Remove from game and read expiration paragraph in Campaign book.

### End of Campaign

Chapter 11 is final act. Special rules in Campaign book.

### How to Save the Game

- Keep card pool, equipment, potions separate.
- Mark unlocked quests with circle, completed with X.
- Mark completed chapters with X, new chapter with circle.

### Game Expansions

Expansions add new quests, monsters, equipment, and hunter classes. Follow expansion rulebooks for integration.

### Hunter's Trial (Variant Rule)

For experienced players. Decide at start of campaign.

- Every failure: mark Defeat track space. Fill all 3: campaign over.
- Every success: reset Defeat track, add erased defeats to Total Defeats.
- First-try success: gain 2 valor points.
- Success without KO: gain 2 valor points.
- **Hunter Score** = 90 − 2×[Total Defeats] + [Valor].

**Rankings**:

| Rank | Score |
|---|---|
| Dragon Slayer | 130 points |
| Beast Master | 110 points |
| Commander | 90 points |
| Prime Hunter | 80 points |
| Expert | 65 points |
| Rookie | <50 points |

### Expedition Mode

Standalone, one-shot scenarios. Choose monster, aggression level, equipment, deck.

**Setup**:
1. Choose scenario.
2. Set up terrain as illustrated.
3. Monster setup: Choose aggression level (0–3).
4. Hunter setup: Choose weapon, armor, helm, item; mastery; build action deck; choose up to 3 potions; apply deck advantages.
5. Success/failure conditions same as Prologue.

### Mount Havoc Campaign

Short, standalone campaign with 3 interconnected scenarios. Middle ground between Campaign and Expedition modes.

**Structure**: 3 chapters, each with Preparation phase, Story phase, Random Encounter phase.

**Preparation Phase**:
- Chapter 1: Choose level 1 equipment (minimum 2 different elements), up to 3 level 1 potions.
- Chapters 2–3: Upgrade equipment, choose 2 equipment cards for defeated monster's element, choose 3 potions, upgrade card pool.

**Random Encounter Phase**: Draw random card to determine scenario. Aggression level = chapter level.

**Elemental Weaknesses**: Modify action deck per standard rules.

**Success/Failure**: Complete all 3 chapters to win. One defeat fails campaign.

## FAQ & Errata

### Rulebook FAQ

**Attrition Damage**: Only refers to damage suffered during Attrition Phase. Damage from other effects (Behavior cards, Unleash) is not Attrition damage.

**Stamina Cost**: Cannot discard more cards than needed. Proceed one card at a time, stop when enough generated. No discard for 0 Stamina cost.

**Forge/Harvester Levels**: Only craft equipment/potions listed in current Forge/Herbalist cards.

**Different Levels of Same Potion**: Yes, if you have unused potions after advancing aggression level. Previous level doesn't prevent preparing same potion at current level.

**Sequence Limit**: Standard limit is 5. Effects can increase/decrease.

**Resilience**: Only reveal a single card with Resilience to draw 1. Each Resilience card once per round.

**Revive in Fire Sector**: Yes, since Fire only prevents playing cards in sequence.

**Vulnerable on Same Attack**: No, becomes available after resolving effect/card that generated it.

**Monster Card Counts**: May differ. Check Components List.

**Skipping Movement Phase**: Yes, you become threatened.

**Immediate Move**: Costs 1 Stamina unless specified. Sand terrain increases by 1.

**Canceling Behavior Effect**: Only skips Reaction step. Rampage cards still trigger.

**Attack Card and Stance Change**: If you end up in protected sector, Attack card considered already played. Resolve as normal.

**Identifying Targets**: Apply Primal rule (worst case scenario). Players encouraged to decide freely.

**Attrition Level at Start**: 0.

**Multiple Wound Cards**: Additional Wound cards useful in campaign. Not used in Expedition mode.

**"Until End of Round" Effects**: End after all "at end of round" effects resolved.

**Empty Deck and Discard Pile**: Draw last card: suffer fatigue damage. If discard pile empty, cannot reshuffle. Remaining draw effects canceled, no additional fatigue damage.

**Mount Havoc: Battle Dance**: Activates every time you play consecutive offensive cards. Can start new sequence if sequence limit increased.

**Electric Whip (Thunder)**: Cannot use on Behavior cards without Boost effect.

**Irden: Level 3 (Potion)**: Attrition phase is part of player's turn. Each player has distinct attrition phase. Only consuming player skips.

**Leverage (Thoreg: S)**: "Max: 5 cards" refers to maximum cards drawn through effect. Hand size not restricted during turn.

**Skillshock (Thoreg: Thunder)**: Second part resolves only if 3 counters before weapon effect activated.

**Spined Armor (Horn)**: Replaces standard Stamina token rule. Gain tokens as described by card text.

**Thunder Armor (Thunder)**: Does not trigger when discarding whole hand due to KO.

**Unbreakable (Ljonar: Mastery)**: When KO'd, fully resolve page 29 procedure, then focus mastery and apply effects.

**"Until End of This Round" / "Until End of the Round"**: Both produce same result.

**At the End of Your Turn**: Resolve after three steps described on page 30.

**Terrain Tokens**: Considered part of Other cards category for Card Resolution Timing.

**Baethanis**: Equivalent to armor/helm/item Start effect. Follows Card Resolution Timing. Resolves after higher priority effects.

**Blind**: Removing Blind token equivalent to Start effect. Follows Card Resolution Timing.

**Plateau**: Stepping off is very first thing on turn. Precedes everything else.

**Synaerea**: Equivalent to Start effect. Follows Card Resolution Timing.

### Biome Expansion FAQ

**Nightmare Biome**: Base weapon damage equals weapon level. At round 4, gain Transformation token. Flip by playing 3 consecutive offensive cards. Cannot assist after flipping.

**Endless Swamp Biome**: Toxic Fume tokens. Draw one per player at setup. At start of each round, draw one and place. At end of turn, pick up one if in sector with token. At 3 tokens, eliminated.

**Goldarks Biome**: Rounds 2, 5, 8, 9, 10: damage doubled. Remove excess damage when inflicting wound.

**Thunder Mountains Biome**: During movement, suffer damage equal to aggression level unless moving with [Dodge]. Rounds 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10: damage doubled.

**Niz-Maraga Biome**: Recycle card matching color shown when round advances. If cannot, suffer damage equal to Aggression Level.

**Sunset Plains Biome**: Rounds 2, 5, 8: all action cards blank. Color effects still apply.

**Woltyar Biome**: At start of round, discard top X cards (X = round number). Lava crossing triggers burning.

**Frozen Wastes Biome**: Rounds 1, 3, 5: mastery cards blank. From round 8: permanently blank.
